      How a coin is sold

      Fake money, no budget. Every scan buys the top pick (the first recommendation) for 50 € and the first 5 coins of the new-launches tab for 3 € each; a coin that is already held is not bought again, and one that was sold may be bought again the next time it is named. Each position is sold, all of it, by whichever comes first: the first minute candle that closes at 11 times the entry price (take-profit +1000%, booked at exactly that level), the first one that closes at 70% of it or lower (stop-loss −30%, booked at that close: a crash through the level fills worse, never better), at the first scan after about 6 hours (at that scan's price), or when the price or the liquidity is gone (rug). A scan without new candles checks both levels on its own price. Failing a filter sells nothing. Paper fees: 1% per trade, at least 0.10; the tip record keeps 0.5%, at least 0.81.
