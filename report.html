Each position is sold, all of it, by whichever comes first: the first minute candle that closes at 11 times the entry price (take-profit +1000%, booked at exactly that level), the first one that closes at 70% of it or lower (stop-loss −30%, booked at that close: a crash through the level fills worse, never better), at the first scan after about 6 hours (at that scan's price), or when the price or the liquidity is gone (rug). A scan without new candles checks both levels on its own price. Failing a filter sells nothing. Paper fees: 1% per trade, at least 0.10; the tip record keeps 0.5%, at least 0.81.
