# What do the 10x coins depend on? (24-25 Oct 2026, fake money only, research only)

Plan and all amendments: [PREREG-ROCKETS-2026-10-24.md](PREREG-ROCKETS-2026-10-24.md), written before any outcome was split by
any fact. Nothing here is built into the Pick, the Radar or the Learning Pick; that needs the user's own yes.

## The answer in short

- **Some launch facts change the odds of a rocket, on months the choice never saw.** On the graduations, at 30 minutes after the
  graduation: a short bonding curve (1-10 minutes), an X link to a single post, no graduation with a shared name word in the 24
  hours before, a picture no graduation of the week before used, and a photo of a real animal each raise the rocket rate about
  2-2.4x (each passes Holm on the unseen windows and on the full unseen month w4). Many facts lower the share of coins that go
  to zero.
- **No rule makes money.** Not one fact x bucket x sell rule holds on the unseen windows; the best is -5.90 EUR per 20 EUR after
  the cost allowance. All facts together (LightGBM) find 5.5 % rockets in their top 1 % at 30 minutes instead of 1.5 %, and still
  lose -2.92 EUR per 20 EUR with the bots' rule K. A rocket rate several times higher is not enough when most coins still fall.
- **Universe L (every launch, not only the graduations):** see the section below; its final run follows the metadata and
  picture fetch of 600,000 / 150,000 launches.

## Graduations: result (run once, 25 Oct 04:10 UTC)

Result file: [results/G_final_2026-10-25.json](results/G_final_2026-10-25.json). Fit windows w1-w3, unseen w4 + w5; a fact under
80 % coverage in w1 or w2 (fresh X post F14, picture facts F19-F22) is fit on w3 alone (amendment 12). Rocket = a 10x close
within 24 hours of the entry; zero = the close after 24 hours at 0.1x or less. Money: 20 EUR paper tickets with the Radar's
fees, minus a 0.60 EUR cost allowance.

**Part A, facts that pass Holm on the unseen windows and on the full month w4 (rocket rate, unseen: favoured / rest):**

Passing Holm on the unseen windows but not on the month w4 alone (so not counted under amendment 7): F15 at 5 minutes (2.39 % /
1.46 %) and F21, no look-alike of a graduation's picture of the 7 days before (5 min 2.57 % / 1.80 %, 30 min 2.02 % / 1.21 %).

Against going to zero, many facts pass (creator with earlier graduations, long bonding curve, X post link, no copycat ticker, own
picture, and more; see the full table). Not passing for rockets: creator history (F1, F2), CJK names, hour, weekend, crowding,
SOL trend, number of links, news of the day, launchpad, description length, animated picture, name fits the picture.

**Part B, money:** 0 of 40 candidates hold. The best unseen means after the allowance are -5.90 (F3 at 30 min, curve over 6 hours)
and -6.13 (F19 at 30 min); every lower bound is far below 0.

**Part C, all facts together:** AUC on the unseen month 0.55 at 5 minutes and 0.615 at 30 minutes. At 30 minutes its top 1 % holds
5.52 % rockets (rest 1.54 %) and loses -2.32 EUR per 20 EUR before and -2.92 after the allowance under rule K (S3); its 1 % lower
bound is -9.53.

**Why more rockets still lose:** at 30 minutes, 70 % of the unseen month's entries close at a tenth or less 24 hours later, and the rockets that
do come take a median 210 minutes to reach 10x; 12.5 % of them first fall to half the entry, where the stop of rule K sells.

graduations (L1, not L2). One in 146 such launches takes off later: a lead, not a rule. The final run puts these together with
the metadata and picture facts in one Holm family.
