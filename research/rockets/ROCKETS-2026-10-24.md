# What do the 10x coins depend on? (24-26 Oct 2026, fake money only, research only)

Plan and all amendments: [PREREG-ROCKETS-2026-10-24.md](PREREG-ROCKETS-2026-10-24.md), written before any outcome was split by
any fact. Nothing here is built into the Pick, the Radar or the Learning Pick; that needs the user's own yes.

## The answer in short

- **Some launch facts change the odds of a rocket, on months the choice never saw.** On the graduations, at 30 minutes after the
  graduation: a short bonding curve (1-10 minutes), an X link to a single post, no graduation with a shared name word in the 24
  hours before, a picture no graduation of the week before used, and a photo of a real animal each raise the rocket rate about
  2-2.4x (each passes Holm on the unseen windows and on the full unseen month w4). Many facts lower the share of coins that go
  to zero.
- **No rule makes money.** Not one fact x bucket x sell rule holds on the unseen windows; the best is -5.90 EUR per 20 EUR after
  the cost allowance. All facts together (LightGBM) find 5.5 % rockets in their top 1 % at 30 minutes instead of 1.5 %, and still
  lose -2.92 EUR per 20 EUR with the bots' rule K. A rocket rate several times higher is not enough when most coins still fall.
- **Every launch (1.56 million, universe L):** for coins that take off later (graduate at least 10 minutes after the launch,
  so others could still buy), a creator's first launch in 30 days raises the chance 2.9x (0.69 % vs 0.24 %), and a launch
  with all three links, an X profile or community link, or a long description about 1.8-2.3x; the last three are marked
  "maybe biased" because the deleted metadata files are not random. **No picture fact matters for these coins.** The copycat
  facts (a picture or ticker of a recent graduation, a hot name word) only raise the instant graduations of the first 10
  minutes, which only the launcher can buy cheaply. All launch facts together put 2.6 % of their top 1 % among the coins that
  take off later, against 0.43 %: one in 38. There is no price for a buy on the bonding curve in free data, so this is a lead,
  not a money rule.

## Universe L: every launch (run once, 26 Oct 03:27 UTC)

Result file: [results/L_final_2026-10-26.json](results/L_final_2026-10-26.json). Fit w3 (10 Aug-7 Sep, 821,715 launches),
unseen month w4 (10 Sep-6 Oct, 739,407). L1 = graduated (w4: 22,244, 3.0 %); L2 = graduated at least 10 minutes after the
launch, the coins others could still buy (w4: 3,230 of 720,393, 0.45 %). Launch facts on every launch, metadata facts on the
files read of the 600,000 sample, picture facts on the 146,901 pictures. One Holm family over all 20 facts x {L1, L2}; a fact
"matters" if it passes Holm on w4 and its rate ratio's 95 % interval (bootstrap over days) starts at 1.25 or more.

**Bias flag (amendment 8 rule 6): every metadata and picture fact is "maybe biased by which files could be read".** The share
of files read is 84.4 % of the graduated vs 61.4 % of the other
launches in w3, and 92.2 % vs 77.8 % in w4 (the bar was a gap of 5
points). The reason (description, not a test): the launches made with metadata.j7tracker.io, whose files are gone, graduate
about a third as often (w4: 1.0 % vs 3.5 %), and the few of its files still online are mostly of graduated coins (w4: 752
files, 14.4 % of them graduated). So these facts compare launches within the readable part only. The chain's curve flag agrees
with the dataset's graduations (w4: 8,461 both, 75
chain only, 0 dataset only).

**Coins that take off later (L2), facts that matter:**

| fact | favoured | L2 rate in (n) / rest | ratio 95 % (days) | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-F1 creator's launches in the 30 days before | none (first launch in 30 days) | 0.686 % (340,979) / 0.235 % | 2.62-3.29 | every launch |
| L-F10 links set at launch | all three (X, Telegram, website) | 1.067 % (4,029) / 0.463 % | 1.73-3.04 | maybe biased |
| L-F11 X link | a profile or community | 0.711 % (61,290) / 0.381 % | 1.62-2.21 | maybe biased |
| L-F18 description length | over 200 characters | 0.832 % (12,615) / 0.452 % | 1.44-2.27 | maybe biased |

Passing Holm for L2 but below the 1.25 bar: a creator with 1-2 graduations in the 30 days before (ratio 1.13-1.45), a CJK name
(1.14-2.18), a picture that looks like a recent graduation's (1.10-1.74). **No picture fact matters for L2**, and neither do
hour, weekend, crowding, SOL trend, copycat ticker, theme heat, news of the day, a fresh X post or the launchpad.

**All graduations (L1), which include the instant ones of the first 10 minutes:** 13 of the 20 facts matter, the biggest
being a picture already used by a graduation of the 7 days before (44.7 % graduate vs 2.3 %), 20 or more graduations of the day
before sharing a name word (12.9 % vs 2.7 %), all three links (12.6 % vs 3.3 %), an X profile or community link (7.1 % vs 2.0 %)
and a creator's first launch in 30 days (5.3 % vs 0.9 %). The copycat facts point at bundled launches that graduate at once;
none of them holds for L2.

**All facts together (LightGBM, fit w3, unseen w4):** L1 AUC 0.88 (top 1 %: 69.7 % graduate vs
3.0 %). L2 AUC 0.73 (95 % 0.72-0.75); its top 1 % holds
2.63 % coins that take off later vs 0.43 % overall, its top 5 % 1.39 %.
The facts it uses most for L2: creator's launches, crowding, SOL trend, creator's graduations, description length, hour.

**What it means:** the best launch score finds one coin that takes off later in 38. It would be bought on the bonding curve,
which free data cannot price (amendment 6: no money test on universe L). On the graduations, where money can be tested, facts
that raise the rocket rate 2-3.6x still lose. So these are leads, not rules.

## What it would take next (costs money or a login)

- X engagement at the launch (likes, reposts, followers of the linked account): the paid X API.
- Social volume per coin: LunarCrush (paid plan).
- Bundles, snipers and the first buyers of each launch: Dune or Bitquery (account and key), or paid Helius RPC credits (the free
  key was used up on 24 Oct 14:19 UTC by this study; the live bots do not use it).
- Telegram call groups: the user's own Telegram key.
- A price for buys on the bonding curve before graduation (every curve trade of each launch): Bitquery or Dune (key), or a paid
  RPC that can read every launch's trades. Without it the launch leads above cannot be tested for money.
