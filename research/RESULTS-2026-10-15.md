# Pick-rule study, 2026-10-15 (round two)

Data: 316,475 learnable rows (liquidity >= $15k, market cap $50k to $2M) in 203 scans of the 2h profile, 2026-10-07 11:02
to 2026-10-15 03:58 UTC (bigtest export ca9c8a4 of 2026-10-15 06:26), each priced again about 2 hours later. 713 gate-passer
rows from only 106 distinct coins; the bot's 191 top picks are about 65 coins. 750 rows carry the new fl.* wallet-flow factors
(645 with fl.netUsd) in 170 of the 173 scans since 2026-10-08 12:10, from 282 coins. Fake money, 20 per pick, fees 0.5% with a
0.81 minimum. Six research agents (gates, momentum, zero vetoes, the score, wallet flow, expected value; about 1,700 variants in
all: 90, about 1,350, 49, 26, 84 and 93) and eight skeptics who re-ran every candidate from its definition. Then the round-one
checks on the larger data (check_gates, check_holding, check_exits) and three skeptics on the one exit result that looked big.

## Findings

- Everything still loses. A random coin beats the bot's top pick in both halves. The score ranks the passers at the 87th
  percentile of random orderings (41st in round one), but inside the current gate its top pick (-2.12) is within the range of
  a random pick from the same gate (-2.42, 30 seeds from -2.88 to -1.49). The score's real correlation with the outcome
  (per-scan Spearman 0.094) measures inertness: its top decile trades half the time and wins 2.2%.
- The 2026-10-08 gate held out of sample. On the 178 scans it never saw, 6-24 h / $100k-2M beats the old 3-24 h / $50k-2M at
  every k (k=1 -2.22 vs -2.46 with 5.3% vs 5.7% to zero, k=3 -2.54 vs -2.97); $50k-100k is still the worst band. check_gates.py
  on all scans: k=1 -2.08 vs -2.33, k=3 -2.41 vs -2.87. No other gate change survived: 30 filters at three k, none positive in
  both halves; letting 24-48 h coins in makes the pool worse (13.6% of those picks to zero); dropping any gate never helps.
- Momentum is a fade among active coins, the same in both halves (per-scan rank correlation c1 -0.17, c6 -0.19, buys1 -0.12);
  ranking all coins by the highest buys1 or c1 picks crashes (-16.08 and -14.65 per 20, 81% and 72% to zero). Among passers,
  ranking by the highest buy share is reliably worse than the bot (paired -1.16, interval -2.15 to -0.27). Several prior weights
  carry nothing here: liqMc (+0.35) and buyRatio1h (+0.15) have rank correlations near 0 or below.
- Zeros are few and clustered: the bot's 9 top-pick zeros are 5 coins (CRAWL, QCOIN, RST, SAI, SUPMKT, 10-09 to 10-11). Four of
  them share one profile (liquidity/market cap 0.07-0.08, +1,900% to +3,600% in 24 h, $1.3-1.7M, no Jupiter data); their end
  prices of 0.001-0.004x may also be a stale price after a pool migration. Near the gate, missing Jupiter data means 10.2% to
  zero against 1.6%, and gt.sellers24 <= 42 means 60% against 3.2%.
- One candidate met the formal bar: passers ordered by the most source lists first (srcN, ties in the bot's order, no
  parameters). k=1 -1.30 per 20, 0 of 191 to zero, interval -2.15 to -0.48, halves -1.43 / -1.17 (bot -2.73 / -2.00), paired
  +1.06 (0.20 to 1.95); k=3 -2.18 (2.9% to zero). It is not adopted: srcN was chosen after looking at zero rates over the whole
  sample (best of 12 orderings), almost all of the gain is dodging the 5 zero coins above (in the other 182 scans +0.09), the 191
  picks are 65 coins (coin-clustered interval -2.40 to -0.14 includes the bot), a Bonferroni-adjusted paired interval includes
  0, and the interval includes the current gate's top pick (-2.12). The mechanism looks real (the zero rate falls from 3.3% at
  srcN 1 to 0.6% at 8 and above): it is a rug filter, not a profit rule. Pre-registered below.
- Wallet flow (fl.*): among the passers only 14 risers (10 coins) against 31 fallers (16 coins), too few. Over all 750 fl rows
  fewer swaps and wallets go with risers (fl.rate1h AUC 0.33), but the DexScreener buy count every row already has separates as
  well (0.29). No fl ranking beat the bot in both halves (84 variants); keep their weight at 0. The counts drift over the week
  (fl.buyers 4.9 to 3.2, fl.flip 0.01 to 0.25): the number of decoded swaps per coin should be stored to normalise them.
  Contrary to the brief, sw.n / sw.avg do not include the last hour's buyers (memebot.py says so too). The holder memory sw.wins
  separates risers from fallers best (AUC 0.82, 0.81 / 0.84 by half), but as a pick rule it only works at k=3.
- Fitted scores: a rank-target ridge over all learnable coins gets -0.45 (-1.07 without two price-glitch rows) by moving the pool
  to old, Jupiter-verified coins after a dip; re-ranking the passers, no fitted model beats the stored score.
- Coins that can move lose more, not less (moving coins -3.65 / -3.79, 10-11% to zero). jup.holders >= 3,000 cuts crashes to
  0.5-1% but gives no positive expected value.
- Holding longer still hurts (check_holding.py, current gate passers): 2 h -2.29 (4.7% to zero), 4 h -2.98, 6 h -3.46, 8 h -3.94,
  12 h -5.70 (23.0%).
- Exit rules. check_exits.py walked each candle path from the scan, about 20 minutes before the buy, and filled stops at their
  level even inside crash candles. On that replay a 30% trailing stop looked like +3.46 per 20 on the new launches' 1 h results;
  the skeptics found 2.94 of it in candles before the purchase. Fixed in 70ffbd8 (entry minute on, closes like the live
  trader). Honest replay, new launches 1 h (n=580): live rule -30% / +1000% -4.52, trailing 30% -4.16, +20% / -20% -3.70; 24 h
  (15-minute candles): live rule -6.80, trailing 20% -5.23, trailing 30% -5.38. On the trader's own young positions over 6 h the
  trailing stop gains about +0.15 to +0.19 per 3 EUR, and the interval touches 0 on the only subset with full 6 h prices
  (n=140). No exit rule meets the bar. The Account page's new exit what-if (896772d, hindsight on the closed paper trades)
  shows -20% stop / 2x target at -0.74 per young trade against -1.07 for the live rule replayed (+0.33, 95% range +0.15 to
  +0.50, both halves) out of 35 rules tried: a lead, not an adoption.
- Data quality: 62 rows show more than 10x within about 2 h, up to 11.8 million (old coins at 15,000x to 20,000x): price errors,
  clipped at +60. 51,950 rows without a DEX listing never go to zero and only pay the fee.

## Adopted

Nothing in the bot: no pick, gate, weight or exit rule met the bar. Research tooling: 70ffbd8 (check_exits.py from the entry
minute, live-trader fills).

The page's line on 2026-10-15 09:02 UTC: "Is the bot making money? No, not so far: -401.15 together on 213 tips sold 1 h later
(4260 staked, fake money, after fees)." Paper account since the restart: -810.38.
