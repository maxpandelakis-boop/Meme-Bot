# Market research: what other Solana meme-coin bots use (2026-10-22)

Question: which data sources and signals do successful sniper bots, trading terminals and trackers use, which of them are
backed by evidence, and which has Meme-Bot not tested yet? Research only, nothing in the bot changes.

Confidence tags: [Certain] = read in the source or the repo, [Likely] = strong inference, [Guessing] = gap filled.

## 1. What the evidence says about who makes money

| Source | Finding | What it means for a 2-hour bot that buys 6-24 h old coins |
|---|---|---|
| Pine Analytics, "Exit Liquidity Machines" | [Certain] Snipers funded directly by the deployer made 15,000+ SOL in a month, 87% of snipes profitable, 55% exited within 1 minute, 85% within 5 minutes. Sniping clusters 14:00-23:00 UTC, almost none 00:00-08:00 UTC. | [Certain] That edge lives in the first seconds and is an insider edge. Our bot cannot copy it. [Likely] The timing pattern is a free, untested signal (hour of day). |
| Ding, Lin, Luo, Xu 2025, "Decompose Market Manipulation Strategies" (6,000 pump.fun coins) | [Certain] Sniper bots in 84% of coins, comment bots 26%, "rat" (hidden accumulation) bots 24%, wash trading 4%. Creators dump first in about 50% of projects. Winners are 19% creators and 16% snipers versus 4.4% creators and 3.3% snipers among normal traders. Winners avoid manipulated coins. Wash and comment bots attract more buyers, not more profit for them. | [Likely] Manipulation markers are a veto (avoid), not a buy signal. Matches our repeated finding that activity predicts a big move, not its direction. |
| arXiv 2602.14860, "Predicting the success of new crypto-tokens: the Pump.fun case" (655,770 coins) | [Certain] Best predictor of graduation is "liquidity velocity" (fewer swaps to reach a curve level). High share of trades sent straight to the contract (bots) lowers graduation. Smart-trader presence gives a small, unstable lift. Creator history is noisy. No strategy is profitable after a naive break-even, fees ignored. | [Likely] The bot-versus-website trade share is a new, free idea. Speed and smart traders were already tested here (winlose, WALLETS) and failed. |
| arXiv 2609.01176, Telegram pump-and-dump (14,499 channels) | [Certain] In real pump events the median call came 4.3 minutes BEFORE the top; buyers at the top lost about 15%. Text content could not separate pumps from organic talk. | [Likely] A Telegram call is a sell or skip signal for us, not a buy. Needs the user's free Telegram API id. |
| arXiv 2607.02795, sniper cohorts | [Certain] All numbers withdrawn by the authors (faulty buy/sell records). | Nothing usable. |
| "Bot made $6.8M on pump.fun" (DailyCoin / Lookonchain) | [Certain] "Spray and pray": 1-2 SOL into many coins, no win rate given. | [Likely] Survivorship story. We already tested many small bets on new launches (filtersearch): they lost most. |

[Likely] Photon, BullX, Axiom, Trojan, GMGN terminals sell speed and screens, not a proven edge. Their "smart money", "KOL"
and "bundle" tags are labels that their users trade on, so they move prices short term, but no public study shows profit
from following them after fees. Paid call groups and "AI bot" ads have no verifiable records.

## 2. What Meme-Bot has already tested (all failed as a buy rule)

[Certain] From research/ and the project notes: social signals (X, website, Telegram/pump.fun replies, Dex boosts, news,
Reddit, trending), dev/holder factors (RugCheck, Jupiter holders, creator sold, top-10), smart wallets from pump.fun swaps
(copy trading), 213,474 filter combinations, momentum, time-machine replays, look-alike charts, 87 chart/wallet/market
features with LightGBM (winlose), exit rules, new launches. The common pattern: risk filters remove the rockets, activity
predicts the size of a move, not the direction.

[Certain] The code already has GMGN fields (gm.smartDegen, gm.renowned = KOL holders, gm.bundler, gm.rat, gm.wash,
gm.sniperHold, gm.devHold) but traders.py notes GMGN's Cloudflare usually refuses the bot, so these factors are mostly empty
and have never been tested properly.

## 3. Untested sources and signals, ranked by expected benefit

| # | Signal | Source | Cost / key | Testable on old data? | Why it might help | Expected benefit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hour of day and weekday of the pick | data the bot already stores (scan time) | free, no key | yes, now | Pine: insider sniping and launches cluster 14-23 UTC. Coins picked in busy hours may meet more dumping. | [Guessing] small, but costs nothing and needs only one evening of testing |
| 2 | Bot share of last-hour buys (trades sent straight to the contract or by known MEV bots versus trades via retail terminals Axiom, Photon, BullX, Trojan, GMGN, recognisable by their fee wallets in the transaction) | the bot's existing public-RPC trade sample (fl.*) plus a list of terminal fee wallets | free, no key | forward only (needs logging) | arXiv 2602.14860: high bot share lowers success. Retail flow through terminals is real demand. | [Likely] medium; the most promising free new idea |
| 3 | Fresh-wallet share and shared funding among last-hour buyers (wallets born in the last 24 h, or funded by the same source) | public RPC (first signature, funding transfer) from GitHub Actions | free, no key, more RPC calls | forward only | Ding et al.: wash and rat bots use fresh, linked wallets; winners avoid such coins. Veto, not a buy signal. | [Likely] medium as a veto |
| 4 | GMGN official API: KOL holders, smart-money count, bundler/rat/wash/sniper shares for our 6-24 h coins | gmgn.ai/ai (official key, no Cloudflare) | free key [Guessing about the price; the docs do not say], needs the user | forward only | Fills fields the bot already scores but never had. KOL wallets are different from the pump.fun swap wallets tested in WALLETS. | [Likely] medium; the cheapest way to fill many fields at once |
| 5 | Telegram call veto (coin called in public call channels in the last hour) | Telegram API (user's own api_id) | free, user must create it | forward only | Telegram paper: calls come minutes before the top, then -15%. | [Likely] medium as a veto; low as a buy signal |
| 6 | Supply held by wallets the deployer funded (multi-hop) | public RPC or Helius | free RPC (slow) or Helius free 1M credits/month | forward only | Pine: deployer-funded snipers are the profitable insiders; their supply is future selling. | [Likely] medium as a veto, heavy to build |
| 7 | Labelled holder shares (sniper %, bundler %, insider %, dev %) | Mobula (/api/2/token/details) or Solana Tracker (/tokens/{t}/bundlers, insider chart) | Mobula free 10,000 credits/month at 1 rps (tight, about 20 coins a scan may not fit); paid from $50/month. Solana Tracker free tier not stated. | forward only | Same idea as #4 from another vendor; a backup if GMGN refuses. | [Guessing] same as #4 |
| 8 | Bubblemaps cluster share | Bubblemaps API | key on request, [Guessing] paid | forward only | Cluster supply = hidden insiders. | [Guessing] low to medium, likely overlaps #6 |
| 9 | Birdeye top traders / security | Birdeye | free 30,000 CU/month at 1 rps (too small); Starter $99/month | forward only | Mostly the same data as GMGN/Jupiter. | low |

Dropped: Moralis snipers endpoint (removed 4 June 2026) [Certain]; Photon/BullX/Axiom (no data API, they are terminals)
[Certain]; paid call groups and "AI bots" (no checkable record) [Likely].

## 4. Honest read

[Likely] More sources alone will not make the bot the best on the market. The bots that clearly earn are insiders and
same-block snipers that sell within a minute. For a bot that buys hourly, the realistic gain from new data is fewer bad
buys (vetoes), not more rockets. Every candidate must pass the same pre-registered out-of-sample test as before.
