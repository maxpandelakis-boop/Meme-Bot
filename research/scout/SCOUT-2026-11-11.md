Pre-registered in research/scout/PREREG-SCOUT-2026-11-11.md (amendments 1-3 before any outcome) and
research/scout/PREREG-CONFIRM-2026-11-11.md. Data: Slinky21/Pumpfun_v2_dataset (CC BY 4.0, "Data provided by [Slink21].

## The video (frames of the Trading Desk TikTok)

- The coin choice is an LLM ("Grok Bot picks every click via the xAI API; X via x_search"), not a fixed rule. Visible bar: about
  5x the normal X post rate (5.1x to 6.4x with 64 to 98 "real" accounts bought; 3.1x to 4.4x "wait", three of those bought
  minutes later). Buys at $14K to $28K market cap: fresh pump.fun curve coins.
- The video looks staged: it shows Solana slot 318,206,567 on a November 2026 date (the chain was at slot ~439,500,000 already
  in August 2026), 6 of 8 buys reach 2x, 20 $ become 268 $ in 4 hours. DexScreener (searched from GitHub Actions) has no Solana
  pair named HUSK, LARK, PINE, MOSS, DUSK, CORAL, FLINT or WILLOW created 6 to 10 Nov 2026 (only one GULP with 10 $ volume).
  The bot's wallet is not shown, so its trades cannot be traced on the chain.

## Verdict: every rule FAILS

Holdout = coins launched 1 to 7 Oct 2026, dev = 24 to 30 Sep; ticket 20 $, FOMO's real fee tiers, 1% slippage a side.

- Dev days point the same way (all negative, 0 to 1 positive days of 7 for the primaries).
- The video's -8% stop ends 85 to 90% of the trades; 2x is reached in about 10%.
- The video's desk replayed on the holdout week (20 $, 40% of free cash, max 2 open): ends with 0.70 to 0.89 $ in every cell.
- Fresh coins are not better: V2 is the worst cell (held to the end: -2.6 per 20). The rug filter halves the trades and changes
  the result by about +0.15, within noise.
- Fee cases (holdout averages): with a Jupiter-like swap (no platform fee, 0.05 $ network) the cells lose 0.2 to 0.7 per 20
  instead of 0.9 to 1.5; only V3+R desk (+0.46) and V1r25+R (+0.22) turn slightly positive there, both within noise.
- Not a single "total loss": on the curve the price cannot fall under the start price (~$4K to $5K market cap), so a rug
  costs about 60 to 70% from a $10K+ entry, not 100%.

## The one hint, and its confirmation: FAIL

V3+R held until the curve completes looked positive on both halves (+2.65 dev, +2.26 holdout, 95% +0.07..+5.52), one of about a
dozen hold rows looked at. Confirmed once on 44 untouched days (11 Aug to 23 Sep, 444 trades): -1.40 per 20 (95% -3.41..+0.76),
2 of 6 weeks positive, median trade -13.5. Without the 10% haircut at the completion: +0.42 (-1.86..+2.79), still no pass.
The bottom-bounce coins beat random coins by about +1.7 per 20 (interval crosses 0) but lose money themselves. Dead.

## What follows

No coin rule here earns after fees. The live 24/7 paper desk of the other thread can measure the Scout live, but nothing in these
data says it will profit. Do not build a real-money switch on any of these rules.
