# Video 3 ("FOMO Terminal"): does a trending pick in the bigger, older band make money? Pre-registration (fake money only)

Written 2026-11-11 before any outcome. What the video shows (thread "Match the bot to the TikTok video"): coins from FOMO's
Trending list, $150K to $2.4M market cap, 4 hours to 4 days old, an "Analyst" score of 66 to 92 (not explained), one coin at a
time with about 90% of the cash, stop -6% (lands at -8 to -14%), about 25 trades in 19 hours. The exit is not shown; the build
thread assumed "from +15%, sell 10% under the high".

## Data

Branch history2 (hourly rows of every pump.fun coin graduated 10 Aug to 8 Oct 2026 on PumpSwap) and its one-minute bars (artifact
"bars" of history-build run 39668736908); source Slinky21/Pumpfun_v2_dataset, CC BY 4.0. Limits, said plainly: bars end 55 hours
after a graduation, so ages are 4 to 40 hours (not 4 days); only pump.fun graduates; FOMO's Trending list and the Analyst score are
not in any old data, so "trending" is a PROXY: the 10 band coins with the highest 1-hour volume in each clock hour.

## Candidates and entry

Per UTC clock hour: rows with age 4 to 40 h, market cap $150K-$2.4M, 1-hour volume > 0, no recorder gap before; ranked by 1-hour
volume. Entry for all at the end of that clock hour (no look-ahead), at the open of the first minute bar (within 10 minutes),
+1% slippage. Exits -1% slippage. 12-hour limit (sold at the last close). Stop and trail are checked on each minute bar's low,
filled at the worse of the line and the bar's close; in one bar the stop is checked first.

## Cells

- **P (primary, the video as built):** rank 1 with a positive 1-hour price change (the next rank that has one, within the top 10);
  stop -6%, from +15% sell 10% under the high.
- **L:** every top-10 coin, same exit. **C (control):** 3 random band coins of the same hour, same exit.
- **Variations (dev days only):** stop 6/10/20/30% x trail armed at +15/+30/+100% x trail 10/20% (24 exits) x selections
  {P, L, top-10 rising, top-3, rank by 1-hour price change top-3}. The one variation with the best dev average under Jupiter-like
  fees and at least 200 dev trades is judged once on the holdout as **B**. That is a pick out of 120, so B needs the full bar.

## Fees (per 20 $ ticket, all shown)

FOMO small ticket: 2% a side (5 to 47.50 $). FOMO big ticket (190 $ and up): 0.5% a side. Jupiter-like: 0.05 $ a side.
Judged with FOMO 2% a side (Maxim's 20 $ budget); the others tell what fees alone change.

## Split and pass bar

Dev: clock hours 10 Aug to 14 Sep. Holdout: 15 Sep to 6 Oct, judged once. PASS (P and B each): at least 100 holdout trades,
average per 20 above 0, 95% bootstrap interval by day above 0, average without the best 3 trades above 0, and above the control
C (interval of the difference of daily means above 0). Reported too: one trade per coin, share stopped, the one-coin desk
(20 $, 90% of cash, next pick when flat) replayed over the holdout.

## Note before the run (2026-11-11)

The script was smoke-tested on two days of hourly rows with MADE-UP random bars (no real outcome seen). That showed the band is
thin: a median of about 7 pump.fun graduates per hour in it at ages 4 to 40 h. So "top 10 by volume" is nearly every band coin,
and the control C mostly draws from the same few coins. P (the highest-volume rising coin) and top-3 remain real selections.
