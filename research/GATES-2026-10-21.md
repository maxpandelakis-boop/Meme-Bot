## Added after the first look (not deciding)

- mc: 104 of the 111 vetoed coins sat under the $100k floor (-2.02 per coin), only 7 over the $2M ceiling (-0.34). The ceiling
  is barely tested. Taking out the floor alone is what the data supports; the training learns from coins of $50k and up
  (harness.py GATE_MC), so the proposal is a $50k floor, the gate before 2026-10-08, and the ceiling unchanged.
- crash: almost all of it is the 1-hour rule (-10% or worse in the last hour, 123 coins, -2.46 per coin); the 6-hour rule caught
  36 coins (+0.88 per coin); the 24-hour rule none.
- The population is coins that graduated from pump.fun, 6 to 48 hours old, without the holder, RugCheck, source and copy checks.

## Added later: the check at the pick (decides against the removal)

The pre-registered test asked about all coins a gate throws out; the bot sends one pick a scan. Replayed on the big test (2h
profile, 327 scans of 7 to 21 October, after LEARN_FROM): the old pick is the scan's rank 1, the new pick the highest score among
the coins that pass without the gate. This ignores the safety check for the newly admitted coins (they were never shortlisted), so
it favours the old picks somewhat.

| gates taken out | scans where the pick changes | old picks there, per 20 | to zero | new picks, per 20 | to zero |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| crash | 39 | -3.02 | 1 | -2.68 | 8 |
| $100k floor (to $50k) | 20 | -2.33 | 0 | -5.03 | 2 |
| both | 54 | -2.67 | 1 | -4.75 | 9 |

Among the newly admitted coins the score picks the dangerous ones: nine wipeouts against one. Without the crash gate alone the
new picks average a little better (-2.68 against -3.02) but go to zero 8 times against once; without the floor they are worse on both. **The crash gate and the
$100k floor stay.** Only the liquidity-share veto goes (it failed its own check; at the pick it changed 5% to 14% of the scans
without a proven gain either way, research/STUDY-2026-10-21.md).

## Added later: the big winners (the user: many bad picks, never a big one)

Over the same scans 298 of 11,635 tradable coins (liquidity at least $15k) reached 3x within the 2 hours, 2.6% of them. **None of
them passed the gates** (0 of 186 passers). Every slice of the data that holds more of them holds more wipeouts too, per coin:

| slice | coins | reached 3x | to zero | per 20 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| all tradable | 11,635 | 2.6% | 68% | -9.65 |
| gate passers | 186 | 0.0% | 11% | -3.82 |
| volume over the market cap in 24 h | 3,239 | 4.6% | 62% | -12.45 |
| market cap $100k to $500k | 3,325 | 3.3% | 58% | -9.27 |
| age 6 to 24 h | 1,753 | 1.8% | 33% | -4.29 |

The gates buy their low wipeout rate by leaving out the coins that multiply. No slice found here has more big winners without many
more zeros, and none is positive on average. The tip record says the same about the bot's picks (310 top picks of the 2h profile):
one reached 2x (SEALS, 2.03x), 18% ended in profit, 16 went to zero; by tier: strong -1.66 per 20 (33 picks, 24% in profit), weak
-3.31 (144), risky -2.11 (102), watch -2.01 (28).

## What follows

The user agreed to remove the gates that fail the test. The check at the pick showed that removing the crash gate and the floor
would send worse picks, so only the liquidity-share veto is switched off (research/HISTORY-2026-10-21.md). research/check_liqmc.py
on 4 November has nothing left to judge for the live bot, but it can still be run on the scans as a record.
