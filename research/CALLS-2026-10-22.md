## The video
TikTok @onlyplus4us, 25.6 s, music only. A phone films a Discord channel "inner-circle-calls": a bot posts "INNER CIRCLE CALL"
cards (score, market cap at the call, "PumpSwap 2-10 min old", safety line, entry zone "only on a dip", a "quick trade" plan:

no wallet is shown: nothing in the video can be checked as a result.

All 18 variants lose in every one of the 9 weeks (best week of the best variant: -2.80). Waiting for the dip is worse than
buying at the call (the dips that fill are the coins that keep falling). Kinder fills do not save it: with the stop filled at
exactly -30% the best variant is still -1.48 per 20; with 1% per sale and no minimum fee -2.67; with no fees at all -2.32.

Why: about half of the calls touch +30% first and book about +4 per 20; the other half hit the stop, and a pump.fun coin
crossing -30% usually does it in a crash or a rug, so the stop fills at -60% on average. A 50/50 bet that pays +30% and costs
-60% loses.

## Why the screenshots still look like "100% winrate" (for information)
Of the calls 2 minutes after the graduation ($20k-$90k): 67% reach +30% within 24 h, 43% +72% (the smallest tile in the video),
33% 2x, 16% 3x. But of those that reach +30%, 98% also fall to -30% within the same 24 h, and 95% are below the call price after
24 h. A channel that posts every graduation and later shows only the tiles of the coins that went up can fill a feed with
"+100% ... +800%" every day while a follower of every call loses. (5 and 10 minutes: the same picture, 55-59% reach +30%.)
