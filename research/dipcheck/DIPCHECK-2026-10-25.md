# A checker for the first -20 % touch: result (25 Oct 2026)

Fake money only. Research only: nothing was switched on, no bot or rule changed. Pre-registration:
research/dipcheck/PREREG-DIPCHECK-2026-10-25.md (pushed before any data was built). Code: research/dipcheck/check_dip.py.
Detail page for the user (German): https://claude.ai/artifact/6Mw5pAicJhC9YkNaUbY1ib

**Verdict: no checker holds. Nothing is built, not even a shadow rule.** The computing checker, the three-reviewer committee
(with a debate round) and both ensembles (AND, OR) all fail the pre-registered bar on the unseen month 10 Sep-8 Oct; all of them
are 1.2-1.5 EUR per trade worse than the live Sell bot (v3).

## The committee

Three Claude reviewers (chart reader, flow reader, sceptic), D0 cards without names, dates or clock times, the w1-w3 base rates in
the brief (committee/BRIEF.md). 277 cases (every -20 % touch of the w4 and w5 P buys). HOLD votes round 1: A 3, B 1, C 0; after the
debate: A 1, B 0, C 0; a majority never said HOLD, so the committee equals the plain stop. Its probabilities rank cases at AUC
0.533 (0.462-0.607) for "HOLD makes more money" (base 33 %); its top 10 % were better held in 36 %. The 7 cases where holding
would have ended at 2x or more (up to 3.7x) sat at random places in its ranking. No API key is in the container, so the reviewers
were sub-agents of the research session; a live committee would need a key as a repository secret.

## Why it fails (likely)

The recoveries that can be seen coming are slow (2x within 24 h is 3x as common as within 6 h), and the -50 % floor on the many
losers costs more than the few fast winners bring. This matches PR #42 (wider stops lose in every window) and PR #48 (the value of
holding is noise out of sample). Holders, buyer and seller counts are missing for most windows and could not be tested.

Note on LAUNCH (25 Oct): Learning Pick's rule is stop -20 %, 2x, 2 h (S20-T2-H2), so even a loosened stop would have sold it at
2x (about +49 EUR), never at 14x.
