## What it means

A KOL's coin does jump: from a copy one minute after the buy, 20% of them reach 3x; from 15 minutes, 12% (a random buyer's
coin: 4.4%). But the jump is the followers buying, and a copy one minute late or more buys into it and loses more than a copy
of any random buyer. The KOL's timing adds nothing over a random buyer of the same coins (-6.24 against -6.25). This holds although the list
favours the KOLs (it is today's leaderboard). As pre-registered, nothing is built: no KOL watch in the radar, no vote in Pick,
no rocket-hunter refit (that was only due after a pass; with 47 coins in two months a KOL number would be zero almost always).
Candidate 3 (-0.28 against -2.61 for the gate coins without a KOL) is the only hint, on 154 coins with a range of -4 to +4:
noise until shown otherwise.
