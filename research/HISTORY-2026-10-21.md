# The liquidity-share veto on June and July 2026: result (fake money only)

The pre-registered check (research/PREREG-HISTORY-2026-10-21.md, commit c7e8c70e, written before the rows were seen) on the
branch "history" (research/history_build.py, build e22ef7b1: the Pumpfun Memecoin Corpus by Slinky21, MIT licence,
https://huggingface.co/datasets/Slinky21/Pumpfun_Memecoin_Corpus). Run with `python3 research/check_history_veto.py <dir>`.

## Data

- 148,233 hourly rows of 5,003 graduated coins (PumpSwap pair), 9 June to 14 July 2026; snapshots every 15 minutes at 6 to
  24 hours of age, every 30 minutes later. The finished pump.fun curve, which the screener keeps listing, is left out.
- The 2h gate as far as a screener shows it (ages 6 to 24 h, market cap $100k to $2M, liquidity and 24-hour volume at least
  $15k, no crash, no spike, FluxBeam-like pools out): 1,163 rows of 190 coins, 20 June to 14 July. 1,088 rows of 185 coins have
  a known result 1.7 hours later; the unknown share is about the same for vetoed and kept rows (5.6% and 6.9%).

## Result

| | coins | per coin | rows | per row | to zero (rows) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| all | 185 | -3.78 | 1,088 | -2.33 | 1.4% |
| vetoed (liquidity 20%+ of the market cap) | 110 | -3.50 | 402 | -2.67 | 0.5% |
| kept | 120 | -3.50 | 686 | -2.13 | 1.9% |

(A coin can sit on both sides at different hours.)

**H1 fails.** Per coin the vetoed and the kept coins lost the same (-3.502 against -3.502 per 20; 95% coin-bootstrap interval of
the difference -1.84 to +1.78). Per row the vetoed rows lost more (-2.67 against -2.13), in both halves and in all 3 weeks
with more than a handful of coins, but the bot buys a coin once, and per coin there is no difference. They did not go to zero
more often either (0.5% against 1.9% of rows).

Beside it: a $200k market-cap floor separates nothing either (per coin -3.71 under it against -3.47 over it). The paper
account's exits (stop -30%, 11x, 6 hours) on the same rows make -3.10 per 20, worse than selling at 1.7 hours (-2.33).

## The veto across three periods, per coin

| period | vetoed | kept |
|---|---:|---:|
| 7 to 15 Oct (discovery, coins 6 to 24 h, $100k+) | -4.67 | -4.21 |
| 16 to 20 Oct (hidden) | -4.49 | -3.15 |
| 20 Jun to 14 Jul (this check, the gate's population) | -3.50 | -3.50 |

Worse in October, no different in June/July. With 110 and 120 coins the June/July interval is wide; it neither confirms nor
rules out a small effect.
