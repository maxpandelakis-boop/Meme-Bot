# Curve watch evaluation, 2026-10-16

Fake money only. The curve watch calls pump.fun coins on their bonding curve at 60, 75 or 90 % sold (m: with the momentum filter,
10 points in 5 minutes on pump.fun's list), checks them quickly (ok, risky, trap, unknown) and values each call 60 minutes later as a
3 EUR paper trade with 5 % slippage and the paper fees. The comparison is the radar's green coins of the same UTC hours, valued the
same way (curve_watch.greens_stats). Data: radar-data at 2026-10-16 14:02 UTC, the hour docs db/memecurve and the radar's records
db/memeradar; calls from 2026-10-15 14:23 to 2026-10-16 14:02 UTC in 19 watched hours (the radar did not run 22:00 to 05:00 UTC on the
first night; it runs around the clock since 16 Oct). PumpPortal sends no trades without a paid key, so the watch read pump.fun's list
only. Numbers: research/check_curve.py, recounted independently from the raw hour docs (same results).

## The pre-registered rule

A variant switches the curve push on (radar_curve.json curvePush, pushVerdicts ["ok"]) only with at least 30 valued calls among its
ok-verdict calls, an average paper result above the greens' of the same hours, a rug share not above theirs and a positive advantage in
both time halves of its calls.

Result: no variant qualifies. Only one call in the whole test had an ok verdict (in 60 and 60m, -2.32 EUR). **The curve push stays off.**

## The calls by verdict

346 calls: 268 risky, 54 trap, 23 unknown, 1 ok (a call made without a verdict counts under the first verdict within 90 s).

- 256 of the 268 risky calls still had "RugCheck pending" at the call: the quick check does not finish before the call, so an ok
  verdict is almost impossible. With pushVerdicts ["ok"] the push could not fire even if it were switched on.
- What made them risky: 101 "no socials" (a soft warning), the others measured red flags only (wallets in the launch block,
  sniped in the first 30 s, several coins off the same tweet, a dev with dead launches).
- 22 risky calls show a sniped share over 100 % (101 to 136 %), which is impossible. All 22 are from 15 Oct 14:39 to 20:59 UTC, before
  81d85e7 capped the chip at 100 %; the value under it can still exceed 1 (one wallet buying more than the supply it is measured
  against), so its denominator needs a check. It is a measured red flag (weight 0); it does not change the ok count (those calls also
  had RugCheck pending).

Greens over all 19 hours: 47 coins, 43 valued, -1.44 EUR per 3 EUR, rug 25.6 %, doubled 16.3 %.

- The greens' rug share uses the radar's own label (against the first-sight price). Judged like the calls (gone, dead or 0.1x of the
  entry at 60 minutes) it is 18.6 % over all hours and 22.2 % in 90m's hours: 60 and 75 rug more than the greens, 90 about the same,
  90m less.
- Most greens are valued 60 minutes after the radar first saw the pool; 12 of 43 were rated green 1 to 9 minutes later, so their hold
  was 2 to 8 minutes shorter. Small, direction unknown.
- The halves: split at the median call time; the hour holding the split counts its greens in both halves (3 coins). Splitting the
  greens at the median time instead gives the same signs.
- Earlier than the radar: for the called coins that graduated and that the radar then saw, the call came a median 6.6 (60), 7.3 (60m),
  4.4 (75), 4.0 (75m), 2.7 (90) and 2.7 (90m) minutes before the radar's first sight of the pool (n 21, 12, 20, 11, 11, 9; all earlier).
- Missed: 1,205 coin-and-mark pairs were first seen already past a mark (no call), against 346 calls: the list read every 15 s by
  market cap misses most crossings.

90m looks best (19 valued calls, +1.41 against -1.61, both halves positive, fewer rugs), but it is one variant of six on one day, and
none of its calls had an ok verdict. It is a lead, not an adoption.

## For the next evaluation (pre-registered here, before the data)

- Check first: the sniped share's denominator on the curve (a value over 1).
- On calls made after 2026-10-16 14:00 UTC only (out of sample): variant 90m with any verdict but trap. Switch curvePush to "90m" with
  pushVerdicts ["ok", "risky", "unknown"] only if it has at least 30 valued calls, an average paper result above the greens' of the same
  hours, a rug share not above theirs (the greens judged by the calls' rule), and a positive advantage in both time halves. Otherwise the
  push stays off. At about 20 valued 90m calls a day with the radar running around the clock, that is about 2 days.
- The user's feedback can overrule either way.
