# Copy trading, round 2: is any version profitable? Result (fake money only)

Pre-registered in research/wallets/PREREG-COPY2-2026-11-13.md (grid and finalist rule fixed before any number; finalists frozen
in finalists.json before the hidden days). Code: wallet_day2.py, wallet_grid.py; workflows wallet-events2.yml, wallet-final.yml;
raw reports on the branch walletdata, folder round2 (report_grid_dev.txt, grid_dev.json, report_final.txt, final.json).
Asked by Maxim on 13 Nov: keep trying until the results are like the video's, with as many possibilities as possible.

## What was tried: 7,680 versions

Delay 1 / 2 / 5 / 30 s; FOMO fee with 20 / 50 / 100 / 200 $ tickets or a Jupiter-like fee with 20 / 100 $ (own price impact on
the curve charged); five wallet lists (two by the copier's past return, slow wallets, big buyers, winners without bots), list
window 21 or 7 days; four exits (mirror, mirror with +50/-20 or +100/-30, hold); eight entry filters (coin age, market cap,
two listed wallets agree). Search days 2 to 19 Sep, hidden days 20 Sep to 6 Oct 2026.

## Search days

Of 6,288 versions with 150 or more trades, 369 have an average above 0 and 38 an interval above 0: 0.6%. If every version were
worth exactly 0, chance alone would give about 2.5%. With FOMO's fee: 3 of 4,192. The average version loses 8 to 9% of the
ticket. By lever (average over the versions): a faster copy helps a little (-8.3% at 1 s, -9.6% at 30 s), larger tickets do not
(the price impact costs more than the lower fee saves: -8.5% at 20 $, -12.3% at 200 $), Jupiter's fee is about 3.3 points
better than FOMO's, holding is far worse than any other exit (-19%).

Every dev winner turned negative on the hidden days: the dev profit was the search's luck. The two "two agree" versions come
closest (a loss of about 1% of the ticket with Jupiter's fee, clearly better than a random wallet, not above zero). The one
positive row needs a 1 second copy with no platform fee, was not allowed as a finalist, and its interval includes zero.

## What it means

On the bonding curve no copy version earns after costs on days it was not tuned on: not faster, not with bigger tickets, not
with other lists, exits or filters. The video's result (130 $ to 8,603 $ in a night) would need roughly +10% per trade, every
trade compounding; the best honest version is at -1%.
