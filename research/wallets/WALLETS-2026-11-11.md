# Does following wallets that made profit before make money? Result (fake money only)

Pre-registered in research/wallets/PREREG-WALLETS-2026-11-11.md (lists, exit and pass bar fixed before any outcome; dev days noted
before the single holdout run). Data: Slinky21/Pumpfun_v2_dataset (CC BY 4.0), every pump.fun bonding-curve trade, 58 launch days
(10 Aug to 6 Oct 2026, 4 to 7 Sep left out). Code: wallet_day.py, wallet_report.py, selftest_wallets.py; run by
.github/workflows/wallet-events.yml and wallet-report.yml; reports, results and the rules' trades on the branch walletdata
(report_dev.txt, report_holdout.txt; commits 9182ea3 and 05da1f9).

## Verdict: every list FAILS

Copy = buy at the coin's first trade 30 s after the wallet's first buy (0.05 SOL or more), sell 30 s after its first sell (or at
10x, 24 h, the graduation); 20 $ ticket, FOMO's fee tiers, 1% slippage a side. Lists rebuilt for every test day from the 21 launch
days that ended two days before. Holdout = launch days 20 Sep to 6 Oct 2026 (17 days), dev = 2 to 19 Sep (14 days).

| list | holdout trades | per 20 $ | 95% by day | days in profit | minus random wallet | dev per 20 $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 naive winners (top 300 by own profit) | 119,290 | -1.29 | -1.31..-1.28 | 0/17 | +0.80 (+0.71..+0.91) | -1.38 |
| W2 copyable (copier's past result, t >= 2) | 1,395 | -1.24 | -1.82..-0.58 | 3/17 | +0.89 (+0.32..+1.54) | -0.55 |
| W3 two W2 wallets agree | 310 | -1.77 | -3.01..-0.01 | 4/17 | +0.43 (-0.84..+2.15) | +1.18 |
| random wallet (every event) | 10,475,900 | -2.10 | | | | -2.59 |

- W1 and W2 are "picks better, still loses": better than a random wallet by about 0.8 to 0.9 per 20 $, still a loss of about
  1.2 to 1.3. W3 looked positive on the dev days (125 trades, interval across zero) and lost on the holdout: noise.
- The wallets with the largest own profit are bots: they sell a median 3 seconds after their buy, 91% within 30 seconds. A copy
  30 seconds late buys after they are out; 8% of the copies end in profit. Their record cannot be copied at any FOMO-like delay
  (5 s delay: -1.32).
- The "copyable" record is luck: the W2 wallets' copier average was +7.88 per 20 $ in the list window and -1.67 on the test day
  (2,275 events). Only about 76 wallets a day pass the bar at all (of ~106,000 with 10 or more events; with a true average of 0 chance
  alone would pass about 2,400 at a one-sided t of 2, so 76 is no sign of skill), and 37% are still on the list a week later.
- Other exits on the same trades (information): video desk rules -1.31 / -0.66 / -0.62 (W1 / W2 / W3); held 24 h or to the
  graduation -3.22 / -3.14 / -5.38; Jupiter-like fee -0.60 / -0.56 / -1.10, W2's interval -1.15..+0.14. Nothing above zero.
- Sub-groups, shown because they will be asked for and not a result: W2 in coins older than 15 minutes -0.24 (363 trades), W3
  there +1.09 (60 trades, the dev days had -1.03 on 16). No sub-group is positive on both halves.
- Not driven by one wallet or one trade: without the best trade or the best wallet every list is a little worse.

## What it means

Copying wallets by their record does not earn after FOMO's fees on the bonding curve, with the naive list or with a list built
from what a copier would have got. Together with the earlier runs (graduated coins 15 minutes late: -3.90; 62 KOL wallets:
-6.24) the wallet idea has now failed on three kinds of list. Not built into the Scout.

## Limits

Only the bonding curve; trades after the graduation are not in the data. FOMO's delay is assumed (30 s; 5 s shown). The lists
use a 21-day record with a 2-day lag; a live list could be fresher, but the record's collapse from +7.88 to -1.67 says the
record itself holds little that lasts.
