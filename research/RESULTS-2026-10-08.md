# Pick-rule study, 2026-10-08

Data: 37,904 learnable rows (liquidity >= $15k, market cap $50k to $2M) in 25 scans of the 2h profile, 2026-10-07 11:02
to 2026-10-08 07:23 UTC, each priced again about 2 hours later. Fake money, 20 per pick, fees 0.5% with a 0.81 minimum.
Five research agents (gates, momentum and flow, zero vetoes, fitted scores, binned expected values; over 2,000 variants)
and six skeptics who re-ran every candidate from its definition.

## Baselines

| selection | n | per 20 | win | to zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all learnable coins | 37,904 | -2.12 | 3.2% | 2.6% |
| gate passers as flagged then | 125 | -2.95 | 14.4% | 3.2% |
| the bot's top pick as named then | 20 | -3.49 | 5.0% | 0.0% |
| random coin per scan | 25 | -2.70 | 0.0% | 4.0% |
| a coin that does not trade (fees only) | | -1.62 | | |

## Findings

- 38% of the rows did not trade in the window (mult exactly 1.0, the fee is the loss); only 3.2% ended in profit.
- The bot's ranking among the gate passers sits at the 41st percentile of 5,000 random orderings. The score rewards
  inertness: the sign-flipped score triples the win rate and loses more.
- The coin with the biggest one-hour rise among all coins went to zero in 72% of the scans (-15.78 per 20).
- Stable in both halves: the $50k-$100k band is the worst market-cap band (-2.39, 3.7% to zero); coins under 3 h lose
  -10.30 (40% to zero), 3-6 h -5.61 (11.6% to zero).
- Holding longer does not help: chaining the 2 h windows of the gate passers gives -1.32 (2 h), -2.72 (4 h), -3.64 (8 h),
  -8.46 (12 h), with 3.6%, 7.1%, 14.3% and 30.2% to zero.
- No candidate held up under the skeptics: market cap >= $250k (-0.77, threshold chosen in-sample, the gain is two
  scans), age 12-24 h (-1.61, CI includes the bot), a volume/market-cap veto (look-ahead through unresolved labels),
  rank averages and ridge fits (-1.53 / -1.64, they pick coins that do not trade).
- The harness let fitted rules see labels that were not resolved yet at the scan's time; fixed (history = rows scored at
  or before the scan).

## Adopted

Commit d8548bc: the 2h profile's floors moved to 6 h and $100k (loss avoidance; the current gate's pick -1.39 -> -1.13
per 20, its coins as a whole -2.03 -> -1.32, in-sample and within noise).
