# A clearly profitable filter on the August to October 2026 rows: result (fake money only)

The pre-registered check (research/PREREG-HISTORY2-2026-10-21.md, commit 6d160d2f) run with
`python3 research/check_history2.py <history2>` on the rows of the branch "history2" (research/history_build2.py, run
39668736908): 2,999,235 rows of 62,600 graduated coins, 10 August to 8 October 2026; 2,353,980 rows have a 1.7-hour result
outside recorder outages (3,155 outage minutes). Data provided by [Slink21]. Pumpfun Solana Dataset (2026), CC BY 4.0.

that does nothing costs 1.62 per 20 in fees alone; the gate passers sit just below that.

## Part 2: the filter search

Base (liquidity and 24h volume at least $15k): fit 8,019 coins at -5.52 per 20 (hold), 30% to zero, 7.0% reached 3x; check
4,015 coins at -5.75, 22% to zero, 8.1% reached 3x. Exit B lost less than exit A in the base on both parts (-6.66 against -8.58
on the fit part, -4.30 against -5.99 on the check part), but nothing positive.

Chosen on the fit part (best of 1,176 rule and exit pairs with 300 coins or more): price up 0.52% or more in the last 5 minutes
and at most 12 buyers in the last hour, held 1.7 hours: -1.94 per 20 on the fit part. On the check part: 722 coins, -1.90 per 20,
95% interval -2.23 to -1.59, 3.0% to zero, 1.1% reached 3x (base 8.1%).

**FAILS** on conditions 2 and 3: it loses, and it holds far fewer big winners than the base.

## What it means

All ten best rules on the fit part are "few buyers in the last hour": quiet coins that barely move and so lose only about the
fees. No rule of these numbers finds coins that win more than they lose. Where the big winners are (8% of the base reach 3x
within 6 hours), the coins that go to zero are too (22 to 30%), and no simple screener threshold separates the two on coins it
has not seen. The pre-registered consequence: nothing is built into the bot; the data stays for other questions (one-minute bars
of every coin are kept with the run as an artifact for 90 days).
