# Do "smart wallets" give the bot an edge? Result (fake money only)

The pre-registered test (research/PREREG-WALLETS-2026-10-21.md, commit 6d06300; the check script research/check_wallets.py,
commit 89f7a70, both written before the data existed) run with
`python3 research/check_wallets.py <wallets> <history2>` on the branch "wallets" (research/wallet_build.py, history-build run
39678879671): 19,950,348 wallet-coin pairs, 4,142,820 wallets, 50,930 graduated coins, 10 August to 8 October 2026. Data
provided by [Slink21]. Pumpfun Solana Dataset (2026), CC BY 4.0.

A copy buys at the last price 5 minutes after the wallet's buy. Results per 20 after 2% round-trip fees, clipped to -20..60.

## Verdict: FAILS

Chosen on 1-14 September (best of 126 rules; wallets scored on 10-31 August): wallets with at least 20 scored buys whose copies
made at least +5 per 20 on average, copy the first such wallet into a coin, exit B (-20% stop, 3x target, 6 hours). Already on
the choosing period it lost (-1.74 per 20, 125 coins); no rule of the 126 was positive there.

On the test period (15 September to 8 October), with the wallets scored again on 10 August to 12 September:

| | coins | per 20 | 95% interval | won | reached 3x | to zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| copy of the smart wallets | 154 | **-3.90** | -5.41 to -2.16 | 7.8% | 14.9% | 2.6% |
| base: copy of a random wallet | 15,497 | -5.44 | | 14.1% | 7.6% | 23.2% |

1. at least 100 coins: yes (154)
2. average and its interval above 0: **no**
3. 3x share at least the base's: yes (14.9% against 7.6%)

## What it means

The wallets that did well in August are better than a random wallet in September: twice as many of their coins reach 3x and
far fewer go to zero (2.6% against 23%). But a copy still loses, about 4 per 20, and the loss holds at 1% fees (-3.74), at 3%
(-4.07), and at every copy delay (1 min -3.00, 15 min -3.44, 60 min -3.12). Of the 112 chosen wallets only 20 still bought on
the test period: most good records do not repeat, they were luck or wallets that stopped.

For information (decides nothing): the ten best rules of the choosing period all lose on the test period too (best: two smart
wallets with at least +2 per copy, held 1.7 hours, -1.47 per 20, interval -2.76 to -0.19). None of the copied buys fell in an
hour where the coin passed the bot's own gates (the wallets buy young coins, the 2h profile looks at coins of 6 to 24 hours),
so the wallets cannot simply be added as a vote on the bot's current picks either.

As pre-registered, nothing is built into the bot. A copy of any wallet on these coins loses 5.44 per 20, worse than the bot's
own gates on the same months (-2.03, research/HISTORY2-2026-10-21.md).

## Sources

Every free source was tried from this session; only GitHub can be reached from it (Hugging Face, Dexscreener, Solana RPC,
GMGN and Birdeye are all blocked there), so the data was built in GitHub Actions from the one source that names the wallet of
every swap: the Pumpfun data set. Live wallet watching would need Solana RPC (free, rate-limited) or a keyed API (Helius,
Birdeye); with this result it is not worth setting up.
