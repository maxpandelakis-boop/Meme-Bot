# Which free filters make the fresh-coin Scout pick better coins? Result (fake money only)

Pre-registered in research/filters/PREREG-FILTERS-2026-11-11.md (freeze = amendment 1, pushed before the holdout was opened).
Data: Slinky21/Pumpfun_v2_dataset (CC BY 4.0, "Data provided by [Slink21]. Pumpfun Solana Dataset (2026)"), every pump.fun
bonding-curve trade; run by .github/workflows/filters-study.yml; rows on the branch `scoutfilters`; full tables in
discovery.txt, holdout.txt, combos.txt next to this file.

## Verdict: three filters help a little, none makes a profit

The live fresh Scout's signal (15+ buyers in 5 minutes, buys 1.5x sells, rising, $8K to $250K) replayed on 51 launch days:
51,324 paper buys (about 1,000 a day; the median coin is 80 seconds old at $11K). Ticket 20 $, FOMO's fee tiers, 1% slippage a
side, the desk's exits (-8% stop, half at 2x, 10% trail).

| | discovery (27 days) | holdout (24 days) |
|---|---|---|
| every Scout signal, per 20 $ | -1.35 | -0.80 (95% -0.89..-0.69) |
| days in profit | 0 of 27 | 0 of 24 |
| trades in profit | 9.1% | 10.7% |
| ended at the -8% stop | 77% | 79% |
| same trades without FOMO's fee | -0.66 | -0.11 |
| held until the curve completes | -5.76 | -5.17 |

Fees and slippage are about 1.20 of every 20 $ round trip (FOMO 0.80, slippage 0.40): most of the loss.

- Two of the three filters picked on discovery by the written rule (D1, D2) did not hold: discovery lies, again.
- No filter is in profit on a single period. The best kept sets still lose 0.5 to 0.6 per 20 $.

## The two that go into the Scout (combination looked at after the holdout: descriptive)

"No whale and the creator's only coin in 24 h": keeps 68% of the signals (793 a day), -0.52 per 20 on the holdout against -0.80
(lift +0.28, 95% +0.21..+0.36, better on 23 of 24 days); on discovery -0.78 against -1.35. Adding the dev-buy rule on top did
not add anything on the holdout. Without FOMO's fee this set is +0.18 on the holdout (95% +0.05..+0.34) but -0.08 on discovery
(-0.27..+0.10): about zero, not an edge.

Why the whale rule works: when one wallet (usually the creator) holds 20% or more, one sale drops the price far through the -8%
stop. On discovery those coins (9 to 13% of the signals) lost 3.5 to 4.7 per 20 $; the trades worse than -4 $ were 9% of all
trades and two thirds of the whole loss.

## What the repo search's candidates were worth

- rug-radar's three signs: "launch factory" helps a little (F1/D3). "No early demand" is almost never true for coins this Scout
  picks (2%). "Dev dumped" points the other way here: a creator who has already sold is the danger that is over; the one who
  still holds is the danger to come.
- qlo's "slow, organic" idea, moved to the curve (SOL per minute): no gain, worse when held. qlo's own claim is about coins after
  graduation (PumpSwap); the dataset's swap table (278 GB, all DEXes) would allow that test, it was not run.
- pumpfun-monitor's stages: mint/freeze authority say nothing on pump.fun (always revoked); its RugCheck call is what the Scout
  already does. RugCheck and GoPlus have no history, so they cannot be judged on past data. GoPlus returned only empty authority
  fields for fresh pump.fun coins in the probe (no creator, no holders): nothing to use.
- MELT (non-commercial): not downloaded; its coins are from Dec 2024 to Mar 2025 and all graduated, another universe than this
  Scout's one-minute-old curve coins.

## Trap checks

- Without the best trade: unchanged to the cent (one trade per coin, about 1,000 a day).
- Rows are coins here (one trade per coin). Per day: see "days better".
- The curve path ends at graduation (runners cut short), as in the earlier study; 23 to 26% of the picks complete the curve.
- Not replayable: GeckoTerminal's $3K liquidity floor, the live RugCheck veto, the desk's max-2-open rule (the desk takes the
  coin with the most buyers; the study judges every passer).
- "Serial wallet" features see only the coins launched the same UTC day (weaker early in the day).
- Holdout SOL/USD and market mix differ from discovery (holdout: fewer serial creators, smaller dev buys), which is why the
  discovery-fitted cuts keep other shares there.

## Live sources (probed from GitHub Actions, no key)

RugCheck's full report (`/v1/tokens/<mint>/report`) carries `creator`, `creatorTokens` (the creator's other coins with dates),
`topHolders` and `knownAccounts` (which holder is the curve). GeckoTerminal's token info carries `developer_address` and
`developer_holding_percentage`; its pool trades carry the wallet of each trade. pump.fun's own API answers 403.

## Built

trencher/scout.js `freshVerdict` / `freshCheck` (used by the fresh desk in desk-runner.mjs): one call to RugCheck's full report
per candidate; skip on a danger risk (as before), on a creator with another coin in the last 24 h, or on a wallet other than the
curve with 20% or more. Paper desk only. Expected from the holdout: about 0.3 less loss per 20 $, still a loss with FOMO's fee.
