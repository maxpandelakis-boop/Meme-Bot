# A learned sell decision for the Pick account (result, 25 Oct 2026, fake money only)

Pre-registration: research/mlexit/PREREG-MLEXIT-2026-10-24.md (commit bb1b030, amendment 1 in f620848, both pushed before any
model was fitted). Code: research/mlexit/check_mlexit.py (build, check, choose, test). Results: check.json, choose.json,
result.json. Research only: nothing is switched on, the Pick keeps the chart exit v3.

## Verdict

- **It does not hold.** Chosen on 25 Apr-9 Sep, the learned exit beat the live chart exit v3 on the whole unseen month 10 Sep-8 Oct
  by only +0.30 EUR per 50 EUR trade (95 % CI -1.39 to +1.90; one-sided 5 % bound -1.11). The bar needs the bound above 0.
- **It is no smarter than "get out".** The placebo (the same model trained on shuffled values, so it can learn nothing but the
  average) beat v3 on the same month by +1.20, more than the real model. Selling every buy after five minutes (exploratory, not
  pre-registered) beat v3 by +1.58. The model sold 320 of 356 trades by its own decision, most of them early.
- **It still loses money**: -2.87 EUR per trade on 10 Sep-8 Oct, -0.64 on 9-21 Oct. It made no trade of 3x or more (v3: 1, K: 2).
- **Why: the value of holding is noise.** On the unseen month the model explains none of what holding is worth (R² -0.03 on the
  Pick buys, +0.01 on 1,263 wider buys). Where it said "holding is worth more than selling" (its top tenth), holding actually lost
  4 %. The only thing it learned reliably is that holding these coins loses on average, which is a fact about the buys, not about
  the moment to sell.
- **The next half hour is a little predictable, not enough.** "Is the price lower in 30 minutes?" was right 62 % of the time
  among the moments the price moved (a coin flip: 53 %, AUC 0.67). That is real, but the moves are small next to the 2 % round
  trip fee, and the big moves (where the money is) are not among the predictable ones.
- **What was on the table**: selling every Pick buy at the highest high of its 12 hours (hindsight, nobody can) would have made
  +31.78 EUR per trade on 10 Sep-8 Oct. The model captured none of it.

## Data

16,801 coins, 56,113 entries, 4.25 million decision moments (every five-minute candle a position is held), five windows
25 Apr-21 Oct (GeckoTerminal w1, w2, w5; history2 w3, w4). The Pick-type buys and their K / v3 results reproduce
research/chartexit/result.json exactly (348 / 394 / 569 / 356 / 185 coins; check.json); the policy kernel reproduces the plain
replays on every coin; changing every candle after a decision changes none of its 82 numbers (900 checks). Of about 72,000
graduations only these 16,801 are still tradeable (market cap 30k or more, liquidity 8k or more, a trade in the hour before) an
hour or more after graduating; all of them were used (amendment 1, the user's "Nimm so viel du kannst").

The model saw: the position (time held, price over entry, highest high, drawdown), the chart (returns over 5 min-6 h, the last
24 five-minute returns one by one, volatility, ranges, distance from the high and low since graduation, candle shapes, falling
runs), volume (5 / 15 / 60 min, against the hour before and the entry hour, empty candles, buy-side share, turnover), market cap
and liquidity, age, SOL (1 / 6 / 24 h) and BTC (24 h), hour and weekday, and the launch facts (X / Telegram / website links,
description, video, name, CJK letters, the creator's earlier graduations, bonding-curve time and signatures). Holders, buyers,
sellers and wallets exist for no GeckoTerminal window and are not seen live, so they are not in it.

## Choice (fitted on w1+w2, judged on w3's 569 Pick buys)

48 configurations: training universe W (3,669 coins) or ALL (10,567 coins in w1-w3); A1 (value of holding under a plain
floor -50 % / 11x / 12 h, sell when it is below 1 - m), A2 (one more round of policy iteration), B (v3 plus "sell now when the value
is below 1 - m"); m 0 / 0.03 / 0.06 / 0.10; a decision every 5 or every 15 minutes. The value models stopped early after 1-93 trees
(the validation loss rose after that): little to learn. B's model on W was a single tree, a constant. Chosen: W, A2, m 0, every
15 min (w3: -0.49 per trade, v3 -1.95, +1.47). The configurations scattered from -5.6 to -0.5 on w3; A2 swings from best to worst
with a 3 % change of margin, a sign of fitting noise.

Holding longer loses more in every window (after 15 / 30 / 60 minutes it is worse step by step). Selling at once loses about the
fees; not buying loses nothing. So no exit can turn these buys into profit; the loss is in the buys (the venue), as found before.

## Value model diagnostics (fitted on w1-w3, judged on w4)

Deciles of the predicted value of holding (B0 base) against what holding was worth, Pick buys: predicted 0.69 / 0.79 / 0.84 /
0.87 / 0.90 / 0.92 / 0.94 / 0.96 / 0.98 / 1.02, real 0.90 / 0.99 / 1.02 / 1.04 / 1.09 / 1.08 / 0.99 / 0.97 / 0.94 / 0.96. The real
value is highest in the middle and falls at the top: no usable order. The v3 value model is even inverse on the Pick buys
(rank correlation -0.16). The largest weights went to launch facts, BTC's day and turnover (which coins or days fade faster), not to
the chart at the moment.
