# Momentum lead, checked 2026-10-21: no lead (fake money only)

The question (the user, 21 Oct): can the radar notice the coins that run, as the new launches seemed to?

## Why the new launches lost less than the radar

Same 30 UTC hours (14 Oct 09:00 to 15 Oct 21:00), 3 EUR each with fees: new launches 137 trades, -0.80 EUR per trade; the radar's
3 EUR test 244 trades, -1.33 (difference +0.53, 95% bootstrap -0.27 to +1.35; older half -0.71 against -1.56, newer half -0.89
against -0.88). Not clearly better. Where they lost less:

- They were older when bought (median 25 minutes): those under 10 minutes old lost -1.65 per 3 EUR, as much as the radar; 10 to 60
  minutes old -0.36 to -0.90. The radar buys at first sight.
- The radar's old 3 EUR test bought mostly coins it rated trap (178 of 244; 68% of them total losses).
- Liquidity and market cap at the buy were about the same (median $21k, $45k to $51k).
- None of the 644 new launches went 10,000x. The highest raw top was 303x (a print during a collapse; 3.8x sellable); the one real
  runner was FLOYD (93x; the paper account sold it at 11x, +28.58 EUR on 3), bought on 13 Oct, before the radar ran. The 10,000x tops
  were in the radar's old test (YOYO 28,021x, SARWAND 17,436x, CAPE 14,722x): prints in dead pools ($0 to $0.14 of liquidity), each a
  total loss.

## The momentum band, and why it is not a lead

In the radar's records (7,703 pools, 14 to 21 Oct; prices 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes after first sight), pools up 3x to under 5x at the
15-minute look made +1.08 EUR per 3 to the 60-minute look (n 221, both halves positive), while 2x to 3x (-0.52) and 5x or more (-2.05)
lost. A review found the cause: 101 of the 221 are one FluxBeam pool family (160 records, all machine-made: market cap 0.99 times the
liquidity at first sight, about $1.2M of liquidity at 60 minutes, copied tickers, 155 of 160 rated trap by the radar, smooth ramps on
about $150 a minute of volume). That family makes +3.97 per 3; everything else in the band makes -1.36 (n 120; pumpswap -1.11, meteora
-2.67), worse than the -0.96 of every other pool alive at 15 minutes. So the band says nothing about momentum, and the paper test
built for it (rule 3 of radar_paper.py, commit 4731cf3e) was withdrawn before it was pushed.

Waiting before buying does lose less at equal holding times (greens bought at the rating and held 30 minutes: -1.32 per 3 EUR, 21%
total losses; bought at 30 minutes and held 30: -0.48, 2%), because most rugs come in the first 15 to 30 minutes; but nothing in these
records makes money after fees (-0.34 of the -0.48 is fees and slippage).

Side note for the radar account: peak.py books the FluxBeam family's ramps as wins (the rule-1 docs PTAV, GTG x4 and PUFFER closed at
the 6-hour close, 4.4x to 6.7x, on about 50 minutes of candles); the radar rates them trap and rule 2 never buys them.
