# Do social signals pick the coins that rise? Result (fake money only)

The pre-registered check (research/PREREG-SOCIAL-2026-10-21.md, commit acadd73) run with
`python3 -I research/check_social.py <memesnapres>` on the big test (bigtest export bcea13f, profile m9-2h, 341 scans,
7 to 21 Oct 2026). One entry per coin and half, result after about 2 hours, 2% round-trip fees (1% each side).

## Baseline

| | fit half (7-13 Oct) | check half (14-21 Oct) |
|---|---|---|
| all learnable coins | 4,540 coins, -7.16 per 20, 33.7% to zero, 0.9% 3x | 3,749 coins, -6.71, 32.4% to zero, 1.1% 3x |
| gate passers | 102 coins, -2.81, 2.9% to zero, 0% 3x | 85 coins, -0.96, 1.2% to zero, 0% 3x |

Fees barely matter here: 1% / 2% / 3% round trip move the passers only from -0.77 to -0.96 to -1.15 (check half).

**No buy signal.** No signal, on either half, in either group, makes coins win after fees.

## Why the "vetoes" do not help the bot

The vetoes hold on all coins: coins with no X link, no website and few pump.fun comments go to zero three times as often.
But the bot's gates already remove those coins: the passers go to zero only 1 to 3% of the time. Among the passers the same
signals separate nothing (check half: X link on -0.61 vs off -1.39, interval -1.57..+3.40; on the fit half the other way round,
-3.84 vs -1.22). The pre-registered 3x condition passes only because the passers had no 3x coin at all. A social veto would cut
about half of the picks with no proven gain, so it is not built in. The vetoes also throw out most 3x coins of the wide pool
(1.8% 3x without an X link against 0.4% with one): the big hits come from the same quiet, link-less coins that mostly die.

## The one hint for big hits (not proven)

Coins whose X link points to one tweet (a launch tweet, not an account) hit 3x four times as often, on both halves
(3.6% vs 0.7% and 3.2% vs 0.8%; 13 of 402 coins on the check half). They also lose more on average. Too few hits to build on.

## Verdict

FAILS for a buy signal. The social data the bot already collects tells trash from non-trash, which the gates already do; it
does not tell winners from losers among the coins the bot may pick. Nothing is built in.
