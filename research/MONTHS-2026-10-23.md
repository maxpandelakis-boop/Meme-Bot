# Tests over months: the bots' rules and about 1.5 million rules, walk-forward over five windows (Apr-Oct 2026)

Fake money only, research only: nothing in the Pick, the Radar or the Learning Pick was changed or switched on.
Pre-registration: research/PREREG-MONTHS-2026-10-23.md (commit 58eb1a0, written before any new candle was looked at; one
sentence on the classifier's scoring was made clearer before the first run). Data: research/months_fetch.py and
.github/workflows/months-data.yml, branches `monthsdata` (GeckoTerminal windows) and `monthsh2` (history2 as five-minute candles).
Analysis: research/check_months.py. Result files: research/months/ (months.json, part1.json, radar.json, persist.json).
Run on 24 Oct 2026.

## Verdict: nothing holds

- **The Pick's rule lost money in every one of the five windows** (25 Apr to 21 Oct, -2.50 to -4.85 EUR per 50 EUR trade under
  rule K), and so did the four shadow sell rules A-D. This is not a bad two weeks: it is the same loss in every month we can get.
- **The Radar model lost money in every month** it can be replayed on (Aug, Sep, 1-8 Oct), at every cut from the top 0.5 % to the
  top 5 %, and on 9-21 Oct with the older model (-7.84 per 20 EUR at the top 0.5 %, research/winlose/WINLOSE-2026-10-22.md).
- **About 1.5 million rules** (2,245 entry filters x 222 sell rules x 3 families, plus a rocket classifier for the Radar type),
  picked on older months and judged on the newest one: **0 of the 6 pre-registered candidates hold.** None passes even the first
  check (40 coins on the unseen window), none has a bootstrap lower bound above zero, and the Holm check on each step's 20 best rules
  found 0 survivors in all 12 family-steps.
- Of the 24 picks made over the four walk-forward steps, 19 lost on the window they had not seen, and the 5 that did not were
  within noise (+0.04 to +9.14 EUR on 9 to 100 coins).
- With hindsight, the best rule on 9-21 Oct makes +17 to +52 EUR per trade on about 40 coins. None of those was picked on the
  older months (one, in family R, was among the 20 best fit rules, with a fit mean of +2.18). That is what luck looks like in a
  search this size, and why "search until something turns up" was not used as the test.

## What "found" means here

The user asked for tests over months and for "all possible rules, as many as you can, until you find something" (23 Oct,
19:32-19:36 UTC). Among hundreds of thousands of rules the best one on the data it was chosen on is always positive, so a
search that stops when something looks good always stops. Here a rule counts as found only if it was chosen on older months
and then held on a month it had not seen, after correcting for how many rules were tried. That is the pre-registered test.

Limits, all known before the run:
- RugCheck gates, the Pick's own ranking, buys/sells, buyers and wallets are not in the GeckoTerminal months, so no rule uses them.
  The Pick replay is its gates as far as candles hold them.
- GeckoTerminal and history2 do not record trades the same way: a five-minute bar with trades has a median volume of about
  $90-100 in the GeckoTerminal windows against about $310 in history2's. Numbers built from activity (candles with trades in the
  last hour, minutes since the last trade, spread of returns) mean slightly different things in w1/w2/w5 than in w3/w4. The
  pre-registered data was kept as it is; a rule on those numbers that "works" in one source and not the other would show up as a
  failure on w5, which is a GeckoTerminal window.
- Entries are at the close of the last finished five-minute candle, sells on five-minute closes. Against history2's one-minute bars
  this changes almost nothing (table below).
- w5 is 13 days, so narrow filters have few coins on it. That is why the step-5 candidates have 9 to 34 coins. Steps 2-4 test on
  full months and give the same picture.

## Part 2: every rule we can build, walk-forward

market cap at least $50k, whole hours 1-24 h, 50 EUR), R (Radar-type, chart only: the Radar's universe, every 30 minutes from
30 min to 12 h, 20 EUR). Each a grid of 2,245 entry filters (the base, 68 single conditions at the fit windows' 20/33/67/80th

tests on ws. MEAN picks the best pooled fit mean, WORST the best worst-month mean, among rules with at least 30 coins in every fit
window. "Top 20 of fit > 0 unseen" counts how many of the 20 best fit rules were positive on the unseen window; "Holm" how many of
them pass a Holm check at 5 % with at least 40 coins.

The months are not pure noise: rules that lose less on older months keep losing less on the newest one (rank correlation 0.58 to
0.86), and the best 1 % lose far less than the average rule. But the best 1 % still lose 2-3 EUR per trade before the cost
allowance, and almost no rule was even positive on the fit months. What carries over is how much a rule loses, not a profit.

The plain entries lose more than the Pick in every window (rule K, base filter): U -10.8 to -17.8 per 50 EUR, R -5.5 to -13.0
per 20 EUR. The Pick's gates are the least bad of the three universes.

## Why the nightly filter search was not pointed at these months

It was suggested that the nightly filter search run over the extended months. It was not done: searching the same fixed months
again, night after night, only finds more luck, and the walk-forward above already did that search once, honestly. New evidence
comes from new days. The nightly filter search, the shadow rules and the Learning Pick already test on each new day, and the
review of 4 Nov (research/PREREG-REVIEW-2026-11-04.md) judges the bots' own coins since 22 Oct. The months-data workflow can be run
again when enough new free days have passed to make a new unseen window (about a month).

## What follows (as pre-registered)

Nothing holds. Across about five months and about 1.5 million rules, nothing chosen on older months held on the newest one. The
bots keep running unchanged on paper, and the 4 Nov review decides with its own pre-registered criteria. Nothing from this study is
built into a bot.

## How to rerun

Data: push a change to research/months_fetch.py or .github/workflows/months-data.yml on this branch (or start the workflow by hand);
it writes branches `monthsh2` and `monthsdata`. Then, with pyarrow, pandas and lightgbm (rows needs about 8 GB of memory):

```
python3 research/check_months.py rows <monthsdata dir> <monthsh2 dir> <history2 gap_minutes.npy> out
python3 research/check_months.py part1 out
python3 research/check_months.py calib out <history2 d.pkl>          # check_history2.py's hourly rows
python3 research/check_months.py radar <Radar grid scratch dir> out  # grid_early_live_tr300.pkl, as for PR #41
python3 research/check_months.py part2 out
python3 research/months/persist.py out
python3 research/check_months.py report out tables.md
```
