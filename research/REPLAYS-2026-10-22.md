# The video's 10,000 replays as a radar forecast: result (fake money only)

The pre-registered check (research/PREREG-REPLAYS-2026-10-22.md, commit b981fad, written before any replay) run with
`python3 research/check_replays.py <history2> <memesnapres>` on the rows of the branch "history2" (Data provided by [Slink21].
Pumpfun Solana Dataset (2026), CC BY 4.0) and the big-test scans of the branch "bigtest" (export of 2026-10-21, rule m9-2h).
Fees as in the tip record (0.5%, at least 0.81 per trade): a coin must rise 8.5% in 1.7 hours to be in profit.

Added after the first run, not a change of the test: the first run was stopped by the machine's memory while reading the scans
(all scan files were held at once); the reader now goes through them one by one in file-name order. Nothing else changed.

## Test 1: how good is the profit chance? (Brier, lower is better)

| | coins | replays | look-alike (forecast.py) | base rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| history2, gate passers from 15 Sep | 313 | 0.2233 | **0.1447** | 0.1540 |
| the bot's own gate passers | 170 | 0.2133 | **0.1258** | 0.1333 |

The replays are far worse than simply saying "about 17% for every coin" (skill -0.45 and -0.60). They are far too sure:

- history2, five bins (forecast -> what happened): 21% -> 2%, 32% -> 10%, 48% -> 24%, 58% -> 29%, 72% -> 30%
- the bot's coins: 2% -> 3%, 31% -> 18%, 43% -> 9%, 54% -> 21%, 70% -> 27%

worse than the look-alike forecast that is already shown on both data sets. The sell rule was not touched.

## Reading it (not deciding)

- The replays are really a momentum score: a coin that rose in most of its past hours gets a high chance. Such coins do end in profit
  more often (about 3 in 10 instead of 2 in 10), but when they fall, they fall harder, so the average does not improve (history2)
  or gets worse (the bot's own coins).
- In the video the method is used for a large share (TSLA), whose hourly moves are small and roughly repeat. A meme coin's past
  hours do not describe its next hours: the rugs and the sudden stops are not in its own history, which is exactly what the
  2026-10-21 pre-registration expected.
- Together with the look-alike forecast (small skill, about +6%), this is the second "see the future" idea from the video that
  cannot pick 6 winners of 10. Any next idea of this kind has to be written down first and judged on coins after it.
