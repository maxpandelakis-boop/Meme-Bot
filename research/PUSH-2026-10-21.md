# Which picks deserve a push to the phone: result (fake money only)

The pre-registered check (research/PREREG-PUSH-2026-10-21.md, commit 14f99ed0) run with
`python3 research/check_push.py <memerec> <tm.json>` on the 2h tip record (310 top picks with a priced horizon; fit: 199 before
16 October, check: 111 from then on). The Time Machine forecasts came from research/check_timemachine.py, unchanged.

## Result, per 20 after fees (picks let through, average, share to zero)

| candidate | fit (7 to 15 Oct) | check (16 to 21 Oct) |
|---|---|---|
| all picks | 199, -2.51, 6.5% | 111, -3.10, 2.7% |
| tier strong | 30, -0.93, 6.7% (under 25%: not eligible) | 3, -8.94, 0% |
| score 48+ | 178, -2.36, 5.6% | 67, -2.52, 4.5% |

| score 52+ | 60, -2.57, 10.0% | 0 |
| trained up at its fit median+ | **102, -1.98, 4.9% (chosen)** | **15, -3.69, 6.7%** |
| Time Machine profit p40+ | 116, -2.53, 6.9% | 79, -3.59, 2.5% |
| Time Machine profit p50+ | 96, -2.43, 6.3% | 72, -3.83, 2.8% |
| Time Machine profit p60+ | 77, -2.28, 6.5% | 60, -4.08, 1.7% |

**FAILS.** The rule chosen on the fit part (trained profit chance at or above 19.66%) let 15 of 111 check picks through, and they
lost more (-3.69 against -3.10) and went to zero more often. No candidate does clearly better on the check part; the strong tier,
best on the fit part, had 3 picks there and lost -8.94 each. Pushes stay as they are.

## What it means

With the data of these two weeks no simple rule picks the better picks out of the bot's picks: the ones that looked better on the
first days were not better on the next ones. Together with research/GATES-2026-10-21.md (no slice holds big winners without many
more wipeouts) this is the honest state: the bot's 2h picks lose about 2.50 to 3.10 per 20 on average, and nothing tried today
changes that. The forward check of the pre-registration (22 October to 4 November) has nothing to judge, since nothing went live.
