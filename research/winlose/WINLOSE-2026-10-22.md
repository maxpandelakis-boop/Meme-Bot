# Winners against losers: result (22 Oct 2026, fake money only)

Pre-registration: research/winlose/PREREG-WINLOSE-2026-10-22.md (commit 0da44a48, before any number was compared with an outcome).
Data: history2 (Slink21 Pumpfun dataset, CC BY 4.0) hourly rows and one-minute bars (branch chartbars), the wallet pairs (branch
wallets), BTC/SOL hourly candles (Binance public data, branch marketctx). Scripts: features.py, lookalike.py, partA.py, partB.py,
export.py in this folder. 24,710 rows of 4,738 coins a bot with the kept gates sees at 1 to 24 hours; 87 numbers per row.

## Part A: where winners and losers differ (like with like: age x market-cap fifth, one row per coin, Holm)

| comparison | numbers that hold (same direction on early, late and unseen days, Holm p < 0.05) |
|---|---|
| big move either way (3x or -80%) against calm | **37 of 87** |
| winner (3x) against loser (-80%) | **2 of 87** |
| winner against all the rest | **0 of 87** |

Big moves are easy to see coming: many trades and buyers in the last hour, small average trades, a straight steep 2 h trend, a
high 1-minute RSI, strong last candles, volume spikes, buy pressure, a small biggest buyer, few touches of a resistance, a fresh
high (AUC 0.62 to 0.74 on unseen days). Every one of these comes before the 3x coins AND before the coins that fall 80%. This is
why danger signals kept looking like 3x signals in every earlier study.

Which way it goes is almost invisible: only "buyers per seller in the last hour" (higher in winners; but no difference on the
early days, AUC 0.50, so the weakest possible pass) and "trades in the last hour" (fewer in winners than in losers of the same
age and size, AUC 0.39) hold. Smart wallets, fresh wallets, the look-alike charts, RSI, candles, support, BTC and SOL do not.

## Part B: the score (gradient boosting, walk-forward)

Choice on dev (weekly walk-forward, 8 candidates): predicted result per 20, top 10%.

| | coins | per 20 (95% range) | in profit of 10 | 3x | fell 80% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dev walk-forward: score | 279 | -1.47 (-2.61 to -0.18) | 2.8 | 2.5% | 14.0% |
| dev walk-forward: bot gates | 350 | -2.71 | 2.1 | 3.4% | 14.0% |
| **unseen days: score** | 189 | **-0.82 (-2.49 to +0.96)** | 2.8 | 4.8% | 12.2% |
| unseen days: bot gates | 213 | -2.65 (-3.83 to -1.47) | 2.3 | 3.3% | 11.3% |
| unseen days: all coins | 1,113 | -8.11 | 1.4 | 11.6% | 47.6% |

**FAILS** (average not above 0). Holm over the 8 candidates on unseen days: none below 0.6. The score is better than the gates on
both parts (about +1.8 per 20) and keeps more 3x coins, but it does not make money, and no candidate reaches 6 of 10 in profit.
The top-2% cut was +7.60 on unseen days but on 18 coins only (range -1.48 to +18.59) and -3.33 on dev: noise.

## What it means and what changes
- Nothing in the bot's picks, gates or sell rule changes (pre-registered consequence of the fail).
- The app shows the result as display only (page_winlose.py, Pick > Details, file winlose.json).
- The score would need live one-minute bars and wallet histories the bot does not collect today; most of its gain over the
  gates comes from the first hour's path, the wallets' earlier results and trade sizes.

## Addendum 1: all signals of the bot's own scans together (combined_bt.py)
91 numbers (screener, dev/holder, RugCheck, pump.fun, social, smart wallets, trade sample) in one LightGBM model plus a two-stage
score (big move x direction), walk-forward by day. Choice "predicted result, top 2%": dev +0.33 per 20 (1,696 coins, range -0.22 to
+1.02); **holdout (18-22 Oct) -0.00 per 20** (417 coins, -0.57 to +0.62), 27% in profit, 3x 0.24%; gates -1.38. **FAILS.** It
learned to pick quiet coins that lose only the fees. The two-stage score lost 7 to 11 per 20: it finds the movers, and most fall.

## Addendum 2: few losers (A) against rockets (B) against expected profit (C) (grid.py, partC.py)
Every coin every 15 minutes from 30 min to 18 h (69,647 points of 5,829 coins), sell rule A from that minute, not clipped.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| A few losers, top 5% (no dev choice: under 100 picks) | 34 | -3.08 | **5.9** | 0% | 0% | 29.4% | 26% |
| B rockets, top 2% (THE choice) | 199 | -2.78 | 1.2 | 26.6% | 6.0% | 21.6% | 21% |
| B rockets, top 0.5% (dev: +8.89 on 28 coins) | 34 | **+16.10** (-3.10 to +38.19) | 2.4 | 38.2% | 11.8% | 14.7% | 99.8% |
| C expected profit, top 2% (THE choice) | 146 | +0.46 (-5.06 to +7.05) | 1.8 | 21.9% | 4.8% | 17.1% | 64% |

**All three FAIL as pre-registered** (Holm over the 12: none below 0.65). The two goals pull apart: the model that gets most picks
in profit (A) loses money because its few losers go to zero; the one that makes money (B, strictest cut) has only 2.4 of 10 in
profit and lives on 4 rockets among 34 picks. The rocket hunter finds 10x coins 6x more often than chance (11.8% vs 1.9%) and was
positive on both periods, but on 62 coins in all: a candidate for a forward paper test, not a proven edge. Nothing changes in
the bot; the app shows all three side by side.

## Addendum 3: a rocket score the radar can compute at launch (radar_rocket.py)
DexScreener numbers 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes after the pool opens (74,551 rows of 27,656 coins). THE choice (top 2%): dev -7.22
per 20; **holdout -11.58** (687 coins), 10x 3.2% against 2.2% for all coins at 5 minutes, 31% to zero. **FAILS.** Not built
into the radar: a "rocket" label with these odds would mislead. The only rocket finder that made money (addendum 2, B) needs
30+ minutes of one-minute chart and the buyers' wallet histories, which the radar does not have at launch.

## Addendum 4: the pick held longer (pick_hold.py)
The gates' coins (first hour per coin), 64 sell rules (hold 1.7/6/12/24 h x stop x target), from the minute bars of 55 hours.
THE choice under the pre-registered rule is today's rule A itself: no rule had a better dev average without fewer picks in profit.

| unseen days (213 coins) | per 20 | in profit of 10 | to zero | 10,000 simulated runs in profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rule A (6 h, -30%, 11x), today | -3.00 (-4.06 to -1.85) | 2.4 | 2.8% | 0% |
| most in profit on dev: 12 h, no stop, sell at 2x | -1.85 (-3.57 to -0.16) | 3.9 | 11.7% | 4% |

Every longer hold lost more on dev than 1.7 h. The 24 h outage filter of the first run left only 135 dev coins (outages cluster in
August); the numbers above keep all coins (an outage only hides trades). **Nothing to switch**: no sell rule makes the pick safe.

## Before a live rocket score (rocket_live_check.py, information only: the holdout was already seen for B)
The rocket hunter (B) refit with only what the radar can get live: 45 numbers from GeckoTerminal minute candles (no buy/sell
split, no trade counts), DexScreener market cap/liquidity/volume and BTC/SOL. Holdout top 0.5%: -4.28 per 20 (39 coins, 10x
2.6%); adding DexScreener trade counts, pump.fun graduations and the bot's own recent results: +0.04 (51 coins, range -9.21 to
+12.38). The edge of B came from the buyers' wallets and trade sizes. The Pumpfun dataset ends on 8 Oct (probe 22 Oct), so no
fresh days exist for a second historical test. A live rocket score needs the buyers' wallets (Helius) and a wallet memory first.
