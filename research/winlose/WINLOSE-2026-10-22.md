# Winners against losers: result (22 Oct 2026, fake money only)

Pre-registration: research/winlose/PREREG-WINLOSE-2026-10-22.md (commit 0da44a48, before any number was compared with an outcome).
Data: history2 (Slink21 Pumpfun dataset, CC BY 4.0) hourly rows and one-minute bars (branch chartbars), the wallet pairs (branch
wallets), BTC/SOL hourly candles (Binance public data, branch marketctx). Scripts: features.py, lookalike.py, partA.py, partB.py,
export.py in this folder. 24,710 rows of 4,738 coins a bot with the kept gates sees at 1 to 24 hours; 87 numbers per row.

## Part A: where winners and losers differ (like with like: age x market-cap fifth, one row per coin, Holm)

| comparison | numbers that hold (same direction on early, late and unseen days, Holm p < 0.05) |
|---|---|
| big move either way (3x or -80%) against calm | **37 of 87** |
| winner (3x) against loser (-80%) | **2 of 87** |
| winner against all the rest | **0 of 87** |

Big moves are easy to see coming: many trades and buyers in the last hour, small average trades, a straight steep 2 h trend, a
high 1-minute RSI, strong last candles, volume spikes, buy pressure, a small biggest buyer, few touches of a resistance, a fresh
high (AUC 0.62 to 0.74 on unseen days). Every one of these comes before the 3x coins AND before the coins that fall 80%. This is
why danger signals kept looking like 3x signals in every earlier study.

Which way it goes is almost invisible: only "buyers per seller in the last hour" (higher in winners; but no difference on the
early days, AUC 0.50, so the weakest possible pass) and "trades in the last hour" (fewer in winners than in losers of the same
age and size, AUC 0.39) hold. Smart wallets, fresh wallets, the look-alike charts, RSI, candles, support, BTC and SOL do not.

## Part B: the score (gradient boosting, walk-forward)

Choice on dev (weekly walk-forward, 8 candidates): predicted result per 20, top 10%.

| | coins | per 20 (95% range) | in profit of 10 | 3x | fell 80% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dev walk-forward: score | 279 | -1.47 (-2.61 to -0.18) | 2.8 | 2.5% | 14.0% |
| dev walk-forward: bot gates | 350 | -2.71 | 2.1 | 3.4% | 14.0% |
| **unseen days: score** | 189 | **-0.82 (-2.49 to +0.96)** | 2.8 | 4.8% | 12.2% |
| unseen days: bot gates | 213 | -2.65 (-3.83 to -1.47) | 2.3 | 3.3% | 11.3% |
| unseen days: all coins | 1,113 | -8.11 | 1.4 | 11.6% | 47.6% |

**FAILS** (average not above 0). Holm over the 8 candidates on unseen days: none below 0.6. The score is better than the gates on
both parts (about +1.8 per 20) and keeps more 3x coins, but it does not make money, and no candidate reaches 6 of 10 in profit.
The top-2% cut was +7.60 on unseen days but on 18 coins only (range -1.48 to +18.59) and -3.33 on dev: noise.

## What it means and what changes
- Nothing in the bot's picks, gates or sell rule changes (pre-registered consequence of the fail).
- The app shows the result as display only (page_winlose.py, Pick > Details, file winlose.json).
- The score would need live one-minute bars and wallet histories the bot does not collect today; most of its gain over the
  gates comes from the first hour's path, the wallets' earlier results and trade sizes.
