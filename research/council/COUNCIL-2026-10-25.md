# A council of 100 checkers that may hold a coin as long as it likes: result (25/26 Oct 2026)

Fake money only. Research only: nothing was switched on, no bot, rule or shadow changed. Pre-registration:
research/council/PREREG-COUNCIL-2026-10-25.md (pushed before any data was built) with amendment 1 (symmetric target clip, written
before any outcome was computed). Code: research/council/check_council.py. Numbers: research/council/results/*.json, the jury's
files in research/council/jury/.

**Verdict: nothing holds. Nothing is built, not even a shadow rule.** None of the seven judged candidates (three council rules per
frame plus the jury) beats the live rule on the unseen month 10 Sep-8 Oct under the pre-registered bar, and none is profitable.

### Why frame B looks better than it is

Learning Pick's -20 % stop is booked at the five-minute candle's low (as in PR #42/#46/#48/#51). On w4 L buys its stop sales fill
at a median 0.635 of the buy price instead of 0.800. The council holds 998 of the 1,390 stop sales, mostly with "second chance"
(RS), and wins back part of that wick: +0.61 per trade from the stop sales with the low fill, -1.45 with the fill at the stop level
(w5: +0.68 / -1.36). So the gain is "not selling into a wick", not foresight. In live trading the truth lies between the two fills.
(Exploratory, after the test: the Pick's v3 Sell bot instead of Learning Pick's rule on L buys is +1.17 / +2.68 / +1.59 / -0.65 /
+1.55 per window with the low fill, -0.37 / +1.27 / +0.44 / -1.90 / -0.02 with the level fill: mixed, no idea to build.)

## The jury (ten Claude reviewers, two rounds)

54 contested cases (w4 and w5 P buys where 20-80 % of the 100 checkers voted HOLD after the debate): 26 floor sales (rugs, sold at
a median 1.8 % of the buy price), 14 trailing stops, 12 time sales, 2 drops. Round 1: 378 of 540 votes HOLD; round 2 on the 48
cases without a unanimous round 1: 376 HOLD votes in total. The jury held 38 of the 54: all 26 rugs (H24, H3 or BE: -11.53 EUR
together), 10 trailing-stop sales with RS (-68.51 EUR), 2 time sales with H1 (+5.17 EUR); total -74.87 EUR on its 54 cases. Its
mean probabilities rank "some plan beats selling" at AUC 0.75 (mostly the cheap rugs) and "the council's plan beats selling" at
0.57. No API key is in the container, so the reviewers were sub-agents of this research session; a live jury would need a key as a
repository secret.

## What it means

The question was asked as widely as the free data allows (every live sale, ten ways of holding up to 42 h, 100 checkers, a debate,
a chair, ten Claude reviewers) and the answer is the same as in PR #42, #48 and #51: for these buys, holding longer loses, and no
checker can tell in advance which coins come back. Rockets like LAUNCH exist (about 1 in 100 buys reaches 10x in the data) but they
look like the coins that die. The single real lever found again is the fill of the stop (how deep into a wick it sells), which is
a question of how sales are booked and executed, not of a checker.
