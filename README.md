# Wallet pairs for the Meme-Bot's smart-wallet test (fake money only)

Data provided by [Slink21]. Pumpfun Solana Dataset (2026). https://huggingface.co/datasets/Slinky21/Pumpfun_v2_dataset (CC BY 4.0)

Derived (changed) from that data set by research/wallet_build.py: for every wallet and graduated pump.fun coin of 10 August to 8 October 2026, the wallet's first PumpSwap buy of at least 0.1 SOL within 48 hours after the graduation, its SOL in and out on the coin, and what a copy of that buy 1/5/15/60 minutes later would have made (hold 1.7 h: hD; exit A: aD; exit B: bD; the 6-hour high: xD; the copy's price: pD; multiples in SOL). wallets.parquet names the wallets with 3 pairs or more.
